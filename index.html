I sit where art meets engineering. Most days that means Spine: 2D and 2.5D rigs with clean mesh topology, animation with real weight and timing, and exports developers can drop straight into Unity.

In Unity I set up the Spine integration, prefabs and particle systems, and I write configurable C# tools so product and animation teams can tune game feel themselves. I care about the quality and optimization of every animation asset, and I work closely with programmers until the gameplay feels right.

I'm just as comfortable in 3D. I've shipped Maya rigs, Unreal animation systems and Python/MEL pipeline tools for Apex Legends, Rise Online World and V.O.L.T. The principles come first either way: anticipation, squash and stretch, arcs and spacing.
